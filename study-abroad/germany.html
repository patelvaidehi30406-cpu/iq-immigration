STUDY IN GERMANY

Study in Germany with IQ Education

Germany is one of the most sought-after study destinations in Europe, offering tuition-free education at public universities and world-class engineering and technical programs. As an economic powerhouse, Germany offers excellent career opportunities for international graduates.

Studying in Germany allows students to gain international exposure, practical learning experience, and access to global job markets. German universities are known for their research excellence and industry partnerships.

Why Study in Germany?

Germany continues to attract students from around the world because of its tuition-free education and engineering excellence.

Benefits of Studying in Germany

  • Tuition-free at most public universities
  • World's top engineering and technical programs
  • 18-month job-seeking visa after graduation
  • Strong industry and research connections
  • EU Blue Card pathway for skilled workers
  • Affordable living costs
  • Multicultural and safe environment
  • Gateway to European job market

Academic Intakes in Germany

Plan your application based on these major university intakes.

Winter Semester

September / October

Major intake. Over 80% of English-taught programs start now.

Summer Semester

March / April

Secondary intake. Only a limited selection of programs accept students.

Work Rights & Post-Study Pathways

Maximize your global career opportunities. Understand your rights as an international student in Germany.

Part-Time Work

International students can work 140 full days or 280 half days per year.

Post-Study Work Visa

Graduates get an 18-month Job Seeking Visa to find employment related to their degree.

PR Pathways

After 2 years of holding an EU Blue Card or skilled work visa, you can apply for permanent settlement.

Program Levels in Germany

Program level refers to the academic stage of education that a student chooses based on their qualifications and career goals.

Available Program Levels

Ausbildung (Vocational Training)

Germany's renowned dual vocational training system.

Preparatory College (Studienkolleg)

Foundation year for students needing academic preparation.

Bachelor's Degree (Undergraduate)

3–4 year undergraduate programs at universities and FH.

Master's Degree (Postgraduate)

1–2 year specialization programs.

MBA Programs

Business leadership programs at private universities.

Doctoral Degree (PhD)

The highest academic qualification focused on research.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about studying in Germany? We have answers.

The APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certificate is mandatory for Indian students to verify their academic documents before applying to German universities or for a student visa.

While many Master's programs are 100% English-taught, learning basic German (A1/A2 level) is highly recommended for part-time jobs and daily life.

A Sperrkonto (Blocked Account) is a special bank account required to prove you have sufficient funds to live in Germany for a year (currently €11,904).
