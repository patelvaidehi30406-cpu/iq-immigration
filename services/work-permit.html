Step by Step

Work Permit / LMIA Process

Follow these 8 steps to successfully prepare and apply for your Work Permit / LMIA.

Get Expert Help
01.

Profile Assessment

Our RCIC consultants assess your work experience, education, and IELTS score to identify the best work permit pathway.

02.

Job Offer / LMIA Audit

We verify your Canadian job offer letter and audit the employer's LMIA document for compliance and authenticity.

03.

Document Collection

Collect employment contracts, pay stubs, reference letters, ECA (educational credential assessment), and police clearance.

04.

Express Entry Profile

Create or update your Express Entry profile with CRS score optimization strategies for high-ranking draws.

05.

Work Permit Application

File the work permit application online through IRCC (Canada) or UKVI portal with all supporting documents.

06.

Biometrics Appointment

Attend a biometrics collection appointment at the nearest Visa Application Centre (VAC).

07.

Medical Examination

Complete a medical examination with an IRCC-designated panel physician if required.

08.

Visa Approval & Port Entry

Receive your work permit approval and get guidance for port of entry formalities.
